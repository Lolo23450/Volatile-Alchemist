Basics • Quick start
Customer order example: the ticket lists required Fire/Nature/Frost — aim to match these values closely.

3 simple steps:

  1. Inspect order: Read the customer's ticket and note any bans (allergies). Check the sizes before playing.
  2. Build potion: Use cards to increase the required stats while keeping an eye on Stability. Prefer small increments early, big plays later.
  3. Serve & compare: When the sum matches closely (diff ≤1), serve for a Masterpiece. Otherwise consider small adjustments or accept the loss strategically.

Extended example: If a ticket wants Fire 3, Nature 2 and bans Frost, plan two medium Fire plays and a small Nature play instead of a single big Fire burst—this preserves Stab and gives room to correct later.

Practical tips: Keep one stabilizer in the deck early, and avoid playing cards of banned types. Use the small Pocket trick (pin your finisher) to delay until the perfect turn.

Stab-aware serving: When uncertain, prefer a slightly underfilled potion over an overfilled one—it's easier and cheaper to top-up later than to recover from low Stab.
